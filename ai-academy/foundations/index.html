AI ACADEMY · STAGE 00

AI/ML Foundations & Orientation

This stage is a dedicated learning environment. Lessons are designed to move from first principles through formal theory, implementation, experimentation, engineering practice and mastery evidence.

Professor standard

Each core lesson is intended to include objectives, prerequisites, intuition, formal definitions, worked examples, implementation, execution traces, under-the-hood behavior, complexity or evaluation, failure modes, exercises, lab work, AI/ML connections, knowledge checks and a mastery gate.
